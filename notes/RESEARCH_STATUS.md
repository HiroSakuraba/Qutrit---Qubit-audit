## Interpreting intermediate code

The scripts are **explicit finite-dimensional mathematical checks**, not simulations of the measured Standard Model from first principles. Later versions revise the interpretation of earlier constructions. Successful regression testing of the scripts does **not** constitute a physics validation or a formal proof of the paper's photon conjecture.

A physically decisive advance would be an independent, source-derived, falsifiable observable beyond the already-known fine-structure value, with no post-hoc fit of normalization or energy scale.
